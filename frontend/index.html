Our story

Slow dough, honest ingredients

Crust & Crumb began with one oven and one stubborn sourdough starter. Today we still shape every loaf by hand, laminate our butter dough overnight and decorate each cake one at a time.

Pre-ordering lets us bake exactly what our neighbours need — less waste, fresher bread, and a cake that's made just for you.
